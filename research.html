Research Areas

🔍

Forensic Geotechnical Engineering

Investigation of distressed reinforced-earth walls, highway approaches, landslides and ground distress; lessons for design practice.

🧱

Earth Pressures & Retaining Walls

EPS geofoam and compressible geo-inclusions, relief shelves, soil arching, and field-instrumented retaining walls.

🏛️

Shallow & Pile Foundations

Bearing capacity, reliability-based design, spatial variability, offshore skirted foundations and screw-fin piles.

📏

Pile Load Testing & Instrumentation

Field load tests, bi-directional pile tests, calibration of earth pressure cells and pressure transducers.

🚇

Tunnelling & Underground Excavations

Deep excavation support systems, contiguous pile walls, anchors and soil–structure–tunnel interaction in urban areas.

🧪

Ground Improvement & Geosynthetics

Stone columns, liquefaction mitigation with tire derivatives and air injection, stabilised aggregates and expansive soils.

Laboratory Development

Developed the Advanced Geotechnical Engineering Laboratory at IIT Bombay, housing advanced instruments including:

  • 100 kN hydraulic actuator
  • 50 kN cyclic triaxial facility
  • Mechanized travelling pluviator
  • Calibration chamber
  • Earth pressure sensors and pore pressure transducers

Technology Development

Part of the research team that developed a process design for the reclamation of waste sand from small foundries, with Prof. Sanjay Mahajani and Prof. G.N. Jadhav, IIT Bombay.

Consultancy

Has provided professional consultancy services to more than 50 clients from industry and government organisations, covering foundations, retaining structures, ground improvement, pile load testing and forensic investigation of distressed structures.
